## 1. Home appliance maintenance schedule

**Area:** Home
**Tool used:** Grok

> "I just used it to create a home appliance maintenance schedule. I just walked through my house and took pictures of each appliance, it found the user manuals and maintenance schedules, helps me troubleshoot issues and error codes, and tracks past maintenance providers and parts that I need to reorder like filters and what size they are. Grok bot is legit"

**What the user did**
- Walked through the house and photographed each appliance

**What the AI handled**
- Identified each appliance from the photo
- Found the user manuals and manufacturer maintenance schedules
- Built a home maintenance schedule
- Troubleshoots issues and decodes error codes
- Tracks past maintenance providers
- Tracks parts to reorder (e.g. filters) including exact sizes

**Why it matters for Roger**
- Low-effort setup: one walkthrough with a phone camera
- Turns scattered, forgotten info (manuals, filter sizes, who serviced the furnace) into one living record
- Natural bridge to robots: the same home inventory is what a home robot will need to know
